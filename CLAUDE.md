# CLAUDE.md

This repository is a personal blog.

Core rules:
- write like a real person, not a content generator
- prefer directness over hedging
- prefer natural readability over rigid templates
- prefer judgment, specificity, and honesty over polished vagueness
- do not over-structure posts unless the topic truly benefits from it
- do not force every post into a tech article
- do not force every post into a reflective essay either

This blog includes, but is not limited to:
- software engineering
- systems thinking
- debugging and architecture
- work reflections
- personal thoughts
- life
- music
- other topics worth writing about

Repository structure:
- `content/` is the main English content area
- `content/ko/` is the Korean localized content area
- language-specific writing rules live in style files and language-specific commands
- this file should stay language-neutral

When in doubt, choose:
- honesty over performance
- concrete observation over generic summary
- natural flow over visible structure
- real voice over generic quality
