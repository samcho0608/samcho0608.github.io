argument-hint: [review focus]
---

Review the current draft for this repository without fully rewriting it.

Use these rules:
- `/CLAUDE.md`
- `/.claude/style/blog-core.md`
- language-specific style rules depending on the draft language
- `/content/ko/CLAUDE.md` when reviewing Korean localized content

Focus on:
- whether the piece sounds real or AI-generated
- whether the structure feels too visible
- whether the point is clear
- whether the voice is preserved
- where the prose gets generic
- where a paragraph should be cut, split, merged, or reordered
- whether the ending is weaker than the body

Output format:
1. Overall read
2. What is already working
3. What feels weak or generic
4. Concrete revision suggestions
5. Optional: 3-5 lines that are especially worth rewriting

Do not turn the review into vague praise.
Do not nitpick minor wording if the real problem is structure or voice.
